Stacks (LIFO)

A stack only lets you add and remove from the top. The last value you push is the first one you pop — last in, first out.

Data structures Beginner LIFO ~5 min
The idea

Last in, first out

Think of a stack of plates: you put a new plate on top, and you take from the top. You never slide one out of the middle.

In code, push adds to the top and pop removes from the top. That’s why stacks power undo history, the call stack, and depth-first search.

TipOnly the top element is accessible. Peek reads it without removing it.

In Python, a list’s end acts as the stack top.

Remember

Stack mental model

Restrict access to one end and you get LIFO for free.

LIFO

Last in, first out

Pop always returns the most recently pushed value.

Top

One access point

You cannot reach middle elements without popping above them first.

Uses

Everywhere in CS

Function calls, undo/redo, parsing, and backtracking all use stacks.
